Our story

A small kitchen with a big table.

Olivo started in 1998 as six tables and one stove next to the fish market. Marco's grandmother wrote the first menu by hand, and her tagliatelle is still on it today.

We buy from farmers and fishermen we know by name, make our pasta every morning and keep the kitchen open late, because good dinners should never be rushed.
